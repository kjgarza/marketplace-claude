## Rental Law Essentials

- **Kaution**: Max 3 months cold rent (§551 BGB). More = illegal. Hard scam signal.
- **Provision**: Since 2015 (Bestellerprinzip), broker fees go to whoever commissioned the broker. Landlord-commissioned brokers cannot legally pass fees to tenant.
- **Mietpreisbremse**: Active in Berlin. New rents must not exceed Mietspiegel + 10% (exceptions: new builds post-2014, major renovations).
- **Staffelmiete**: Annual steps must be in writing upfront. No legal cap on steps but Mietpreisbremse applies to the starting rent.
- **Indexmiete**: Rent tied to national CPI. Legal but protects against spike risk.
- **WBS**: Social housing permit. Not applicable to Kristian.

## Timing

- Berlin listings for quality flats go in 2–4 hours. Speed matters.
- ImmoScout: continuous updates. Kleinanzeigen: continuous. Inberlin: weekday mornings.
- Contract type: always request unbefristet (unlimited). Avoid Zwischenmiete unless explicitly acceptable.
