# Outreach Templates

Message templates for LinkedIn contact outreach. All connection request messages must be ≤300 characters.

---

## Connection Request Messages

### P1 — Hiring Manager / Direct Manager

Goal: Show genuine interest in the team's work, not desperation for the job.

**Template A — Team curiosity:**
> Hi [Name], I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific project/product] and would love to connect. I'm exploring roles in [area] and think there's a strong overlap with my background in [skill].

**Template B — Shared challenge:**
> Hi [Name], I'm really interested in how [Company] approaches [technical area]. I've been working on similar problems at [current/past company] and would love to exchange ideas.

**Template C — Role specific:**
> Hi [Name], I came across the [Job Title] opening at [Company] and your team's work caught my attention. I'd love to connect — happy to share more about my background if helpful.

---

### P2 — Team Peer

Goal: Peer-to-peer tone, shared craft or problem space.

**Template A — Craft-first:**
> Hi [Name], your background in [specific skill/area] at [Company] is impressive. I'm exploring similar work and would love to connect with someone doing it well.

**Template B — Adjacent experience:**
> Hi [Name], I've been working on [similar problem] and noticed [Company] is tackling it in an interesting way. Would love to connect and learn more about your experience there.

**Template C — Direct mutual:**
> Hi [Name], I'm considering applying for the [Job Title] role at [Company] and think we may have overlapping experiences in [area]. Would love to connect.

---

### P3 — Internal Recruiter

Goal: Direct and efficient — recruiters are busy, make the value clear fast.

**Template A — Active applicant:**
> Hi [Name], I recently applied for the [Job Title] role at [Company] and wanted to connect directly. Happy to share my background or answer any questions.

**Template B — Proactive reach:**
> Hi [Name], I'm very interested in [Company]'s [team/product area] and noticed the [Job Title] opening. Would love to be on your radar — happy to share my experience.

---

## Follow-Up Messages (After Connection Accepted)

Send within 48 hours of acceptance. Keep it brief and low-pressure.

### For P1 (Manager):
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I'm genuinely excited about [Company]'s work on [specific thing]. If you have 15 minutes, I'd love to learn more about the team's direction and share a bit about my background. No pressure at all — happy to keep it to a quick exchange.

### For P2 (Peer):
> Thanks for connecting! I'd love to hear what it's like working on [area] at [Company]. Open to a quick chat if you're up for it — always great to connect with people doing similar work.

### For P3 (Recruiter):
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I submitted my application for the [Job Title] role and wanted to flag that I'm genuinely excited about this opportunity. Happy to provide any additional context on my background. Looking forward to hearing about next steps.

---

## Referral Ask (When a Connection Knows Someone at the Company)

Use when you have a 2nd-degree connection. Send to your mutual contact first.

> Hi [Mutual Contact], I hope you're doing well! I noticed you're connected with [Target Name] at [Company]. I'm exploring the [Job Title] opening there and think it's a great fit. Would you be comfortable making a quick introduction? No worries if not — just thought it was worth asking.

---

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending any message, replace every placeholder and verify:
- [ ] [Name] → actual first name
- [ ] [Company] → exact company name
- [ ] [Job Title] → exact role name from the job posting
- [ ] [specific project/product] → something real from their company (product launch, blog post, GitHub repo, news article)
- [ ] [skill/area] → matches the job and your actual background
- [ ] Message is ≤300 characters for connection requests

**Quick personalization sources:**
- Company blog / engineering blog
- Recent news or funding announcements
- Their LinkedIn "About" section or recent posts
- The job description itself (mention something specific from it)
- GitHub repos or open source contributions

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't copy-paste the same message to multiple people at the same company
- Don't mention salary, benefits, or "urgency" to get hired
- Don't write walls of text — shorter is better
- Don't be overly formal ("Dear Sir/Madam") or overly casual ("Hey!")
- Don't send a follow-up the same day as the connection request
