# Pension Tax Treatment Methodology

Detailed calculation methodology for evaluating tax implications of each pension scenario under current German tax law.

## Riester-Rente

- **Contribution phase**: Grundzulage (175 EUR/year) plus Kinderzulage (300 EUR per child born after 2008, 185 EUR before). Sonderausgabenabzug up to 2,100 EUR/year — calculate the actual tax benefit based on the marginal tax rate from employment data.
- **Payout phase**: Full taxation as Sonstige Einkünfte at the personal income tax rate in retirement. Estimate the effective tax rate in retirement (typically lower than during working years).
- **Net benefit**: Calculate the total Zulagen and tax savings over the remaining term, then subtract the projected tax burden in retirement.

## Rürup-Rente (Basisrente)

- **Contribution phase**: Contributions deductible as Sonderausgaben (currently ~100% deductible, up to the annual maximum of approximately 27,566 EUR for singles / 55,132 EUR for married couples in 2025). Calculate actual tax savings.
- **Payout phase**: Taxable portion depends on the year retirement begins (rising to 100% by 2058). Calculate the applicable percentage.
- **Net benefit**: Compare total tax savings during contribution phase against total tax paid during payout phase.

## Private Rentenversicherung

- **Contribution phase**: No tax deductibility for contributions.
- **Payout phase**: Ertragsanteilbesteuerung — only the earnings portion of the pension is taxed, with the taxable share determined by age at retirement start (e.g., 18% at age 67). This is a significant tax advantage.

## Self-Directed ETF Portfolio

- **During accumulation**: Vorabpauschale (advance flat-rate tax) applies annually on unrealized gains, though often minimal.
- **On withdrawal**: Kapitalertragsteuer at 25% plus 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag (total ~26.375%), potentially plus Kirchensteuer. Apply the Sparerpauschbetrag (1,000 EUR single / 2,000 EUR married). Note the Teilfreistellung of 30% for equity ETFs (only 70% of gains are taxable, effective rate ~18.46%).
- **Flexibility advantage**: No lock-in period, full access to capital at any time.
