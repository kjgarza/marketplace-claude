# House vs. Apartment: Cost Structure and Decision Framework

Reference material for house-specific analysis in the real estate readiness assessment.

---

## Cost Structure Differences

### Land Value Ratio (Grundstücksanteil)

The land-to-building ratio affects AfA (depreciation) calculations and total cost modeling.

| Property Type | Typical Land Ratio (Berlin) | Typical Land Ratio (Brandenburg) |
|--------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Eigentumswohnung (apartment) | 20–40% | 15–30% |
| Einfamilienhaus (detached house) | 40–60% | 30–50% |
| Doppelhaushälfte (semi-detached) | 35–50% | 25–40% |
| Reihenhaus (terraced house) | 30–45% | 20–35% |

Source for specific property: check the Bodenrichtwert (standard land value) for the plot location via the BORIS portal (Bodenrichtwertinformationssystem). Calculate: land value = Bodenrichtwert × plot size in m². Subtract from total purchase price to get building value.

### Monthly Running Costs

**Apartments** — costs flow through Hausgeld paid to the Wohnungseigentümergemeinschaft (WEG):
- Hausgeld: EUR 2.50–4.50/m²/month (includes Instandhaltungsrücklage, building insurance, common area maintenance, property management, water, waste)
- Owner pays additionally: Grundsteuer (often included in Hausgeld), contents insurance

**Houses** — owner manages all costs directly:
- Wohngebäudeversicherung (building insurance): EUR 150–500/year depending on size and construction
- Grundsteuer: varies by municipality; typically EUR 200–800/year for a house
- Maintenance reserve: recommend 1–2% of property value per year (no WEG to enforce this — discipline required)
- Garden maintenance: EUR 0 (DIY) to EUR 100–300/month (professional service)
- Heating system maintenance: EUR 150–300/year (Schornsteinfeger mandatory, plus boiler service)
- Water, waste, sewage: billed directly by municipality
- Snow removal / Winterdienst: owner's legal obligation (Räumpflicht)

### One-Time Purchase Costs

Same Kaufnebenkosten structure (Grunderwerbsteuer, Notar, Grundbuch, Makler) but with additions for houses:

| Additional Cost | Applies To | Range |
|----------------|-----------|-------|
| Erschließungskosten (utility connections) | Neubau on undeveloped land | EUR 10,000–30,000 |
| Vermessungskosten (surveying) | If plot boundaries unclear | EUR 1,500–3,000 |
| Baugutachten (building survey) | Bestand houses — strongly recommended | EUR 400–1,000 |
| Energieausweis (energy certificate) | Required for all transactions | EUR 50–500 (Bedarfsausweis more expensive) |

## Sanierung (Renovation) Categories

For Bestand houses, renovation costs depend heavily on condition. Categories:

### Cosmetic (EUR 200–500/m²)
- New paint, wallpaper, flooring
- Kitchen and bathroom refresh (not full replacement)
- Minor repairs, fixture updates

### Moderate (EUR 500–1,000/m²)
- New kitchen and bathrooms
- Electrical system update
- New windows
- Partial insulation (e.g., roof or top floor ceiling)
- New heating system

### Structural (EUR 1,000–1,500/m²)
- Full energetic renovation (Kernsanierung)
- Roof replacement
- Complete insulation (WDVS / Wärmedämmverbundsystem)
- Foundation work, damp proofing
- Load-bearing wall modifications

### GEG Compliance (Gebäudeenergiegesetz)
Since 2024, the GEG imposes obligations on new owners of Bestand properties:
- Heating system: if the existing system is older than 30 years (fossil fuel), it must be replaced. New systems must use at least 65% renewable energy.
- Insulation: top floor ceiling insulation is mandatory if not already present.
- Timeline: obligations typically apply within 2 years of ownership transfer.
- Search for "GEG Pflichten Hauskauf" for current enforcement status.

### KfW Subsidies for Renovation
- KfW 261/262: Subsidized loans for energy-efficient renovation (Effizienzhaussanierung)
- BAFA: Grants for individual measures (heating, insulation, windows)
- Search for "KfW Förderung Sanierung aktuell" for current programs and conditions

## Neubau vs. Bestand Decision Framework

| Factor | Neubau | Bestand |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Base price per m² | Higher (EUR 4,000–6,000+ in Berlin area) | Lower but variable by condition |
| Makler commission | Usually none (developer direct sales) | ~3.57% buyer's share |
| Move-in condition | Immediate, no renovation needed | May require Sanierung |
| Energy efficiency | GEG-compliant, low running costs | Often poor; renovation may be required |
| AfA rate | 3% linear (buildings completed from 2023) | 2% linear (buildings completed before 2023) |
| Layout customization | Often possible during planning phase | Limited by existing structure |
| Timeline risk | Construction delays common (6–24 months) | Immediate availability |
| Hidden defects | Builder warranty (Gewährleistung, 5 years BGB) | Caveat emptor — Baugutachten recommended |
| Location availability | Typically outskirts or Brandenburg | Wider selection in established neighborhoods |

## Berlin/Brandenburg House Market Context (Speckgürtel)

The Speckgürtel (commuter belt) around Berlin offers significantly lower prices for houses compared to Berlin proper. Key areas:

**Popular house-buying areas:**
- **Potsdam**: Most expensive Brandenburg option; university city, good infrastructure
- **Falkensee / Dallgow-Döberitz**: Popular with families; S-Bahn connection via Spandau
- **Bernau / Panketal**: Northeast commuter corridor; S2/RE line
- **Teltow / Kleinmachnow / Stahnsdorf**: Southwest corridor; proximity to Berlin-Steglitz
- **Oranienburg / Hohen Neuendorf**: North corridor; S1 line
- **Königs Wusterhausen / Wildau**: Southeast; RE/S-Bahn connection

**Key considerations:**
- Brandenburg Grunderwerbsteuer is 6.5% (vs. Berlin 6.0%)
- Commute time and cost must be factored into the rent-vs-buy comparison
- Price trends in the Speckgürtel have outpaced Berlin in some recent years — search for current data
- Infrastructure quality varies significantly between municipalities (schools, internet, public transport)
- Check Bebauungsplan (zoning plan) for any planned developments that could affect the area positively or negatively
